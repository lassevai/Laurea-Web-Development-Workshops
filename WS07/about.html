About me

Hey there! 👋 I'm Aurora, a photographer, dancer, and outdoor explorer based in Kuopio. I draw inspiration for my photos from nature, people, and movement. My specialties include portraits, weddings, dance, and natural landscapes.

I mainly work with natural light outdoors, capturing the atmosphere and environment. My goal is always to make your session relaxed and genuine, documenting authentic moments and important life chapters.

If you love candid, natural-feeling photos, and you like my style, shoot me a message.
